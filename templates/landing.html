What is BridgeHive?

BridgeHive is a dedicated web platform designed to bridge the intergenerational gap in Singapore. By utilizing a unique course-based skillsharing model, we connect youth and seniors to foster mutual learning, digital literacy, and community connection.

The Vision In an increasingly digital landscape, BridgeHive serves as the essential, sustainable, and safe digital infrastructure for intergenerational learning. We empower seniors to share valuable life skills while enabling youth to provide essential technology mentorship.
