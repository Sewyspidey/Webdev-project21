Instructor Guidelines

Thank you for contributing a course to BridgeHive. Please ensure:

  • Your content is accurate, respectful, and appropriate for all ages.
  • Materials do not contain profanity, discrimination, violence, or other prohibited topics.
  • You credit any external resources or collaborators.
  • You follow the community standards and refrain from advertising or political content.
  • If you include media (images, videos), you have the rights to share them.
  • You keep the course objective clear and structure modules in logical order.

BridgeHive reserves the right to remove or edit any course that violates these guidelines.
