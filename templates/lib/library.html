How to Use BridgeHive Courses

Overview

Each course is divided into modules (lessons) that you learn sequentially. You must complete all modules before you can take the quiz. After passing the quiz (75%+), you'll receive a certificate.

The Learning Flow
1. Select Course
2. Click "Continue Learning"
3. View All Modules
4. Click Module
5. Watch & Learn
6. Mark Complete
7. Take Quiz
8. Get Certificate
Step-by-Step Guide
Find a Course

Click on any course card to view its details, description, and "Continue Learning" button.

View Modules Page

Click "Continue Learning" to see all course modules in a learning path. Each module shows its status: ✓ Completed, ● In Progress, or 🔒 Locked.

Click a Module to Learn

Open any module card to watch the video lesson, read content, and access additional resources (PDFs, articles, related videos).

Mark Module Complete

Click the "Mark as Complete" button to save your progress. The progress bar updates in real-time.

Complete All Modules

Navigate through all modules using Previous/Next buttons. You must complete every module to unlock the quiz.

Take the Quiz

Once all modules are complete, the "Take Quiz" button becomes active. You need to score at least 75% to pass.

Receive Certificate

Pass the quiz and get your personalized certificate with your name and course completion date!

Important Notes

Quiz is Locked Until All Modules Complete

You cannot take the quiz until you finish every single module. This ensures you have all the knowledge needed to pass!

Real-Time Progress Updates

Your progress is saved instantly. Return anytime to continue where you left off.

Pass Rate: 75% Required

You need to score at least 75% on the quiz to pass the course and earn your certificate.
