The Complete Guide to Peranakan Cuisine History

Peranakan cuisine blends Chinese, Malay, and regional influences. It emerged from cultural intermarriage and trade, producing bold spice pastes (rempah), rich coconut gravies, and carefully balanced flavors.

Key Historical Highlights

  • Origins in the Straits Settlements and coastal trade cities
  • Fusion of Chinese techniques with Malay ingredients
  • Signature dishes like ayam buah keluak and laksa

Flavor Foundations

Rempah is the heart of many dishes. Aromatics are ground and slowly fried to release fragrance before building the final curry or stew.
